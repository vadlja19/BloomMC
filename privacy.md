Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect
Bloom MC collects only the operational data necessary to run the Minecraft server, synchronize linked Discord roles, and moderate community channels. We never sell, rent, or broker user data to third parties.

Data Category	Purpose	Retention
Discord User ID & Role IDs	Account linking (/link), rank role sync, daily cooldowns, and staff panel permissions	Until unlinked or removal request
Minecraft UUID, IGN & IP	World saves, GriefPrevention land claims, anti-cheat logs, and alt-evasion checks	Active season + moderation archive
Support Tickets & Appeals	Resolving bug reports, player disputes, and ban appeal reviews	Restricted to authorized staff
Session Cookie	paradise_panel_session (HttpOnly) used solely for staff signed into /panel	Expires automatically after 12 hours
2. Where Data Is Stored
Bot records, ticket transcripts, and world files are stored directly on our protected server infrastructure and are never uploaded to public pastebins or external ad trackers.

3. Data Access & Deletion Requests
Players or parents may request a copy or deletion of linked Discord/appeal records by opening a ticket in Discord or emailing support@bloommc.xyz.
